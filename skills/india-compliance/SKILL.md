# India compliance defaults

Apply these unless the founder says otherwise. This is product guidance, not
legal advice; flag anything high-stakes for a lawyer.

## Money
- Show prices in INR first (₹), USD second. State whether GST is included.
- GST on software services is 18%; IT services typically use SAC 9983.
- Invoices need the supplier's GSTIN when registered, place of supply, and
  CGST+SGST (intra-state) or IGST (inter-state) split.

## Privacy (DPDP Act 2023)
- Collect only data the feature needs; say why, in plain language, at the
  point of collection.
- Consent must be free, specific and withdrawable as easily as it was given.
- Provide a way to access, correct and erase personal data, and name a
  grievance contact.
- Report personal data breaches; keep a record of processing.

## Contracts (Indian Contract Act 1872)
- Clear offer, acceptance, consideration and parties' capacity.
- Governing law India; default jurisdiction Mumbai courts unless chosen otherwise.
- Payment terms, late-payment interest, IP assignment on full payment,
  termination notice, confidentiality.

## Payments
- Prefer UPI and Indian cards through an RBI-compliant gateway; never store
  card numbers yourself.
