# semgrep and litellm are not needed to answer a request. Versions are
# pinned to a floor matching what this repo is developed against.
#
# Keep in step with pyproject.toml's [project.optional-dependencies].

# mesh/store.py — command_threads / artifacts / dashboard_events write-through.
